TITLE: E komo mai… welcome in

Onaona i ka hala me ka lehua … he hale lehua nō ia na ka noe…ʻO kaʻu nō ia e ʻanoʻi nei, e liʻa nei hoʻi o ka hiki mai…a hiki mai nō ʻoukou. A hiki pū nō me ke aloha… Aloha ē… aloha ē… aloha ē… 
 -he mele aloha, a chant used, in Hawaiʻi, to share aloha with others… 
 Welcome to WalkPono LLC! We are delighted to have you here. 
 At WalkPono, we believe in the transformative power of mindfulness and forgiveness…especially when grounded in the powerful foundations of the mo’omeheu Hawai’i  and nohona Hawai’i  (Hawaiian culture). Our mission is to accompany individuals and groups on a journey of self-discovery, healing, and personal growth. 
 In today’s fast-paced world, finding moments of stillness and cultivating a compassionate mindset can be challenging. That’s why we are dedicated to providing you with the tools, techniques, and support needed to navigate life’s challenges with grace and resilience. 
 Forgiveness is at the core of our work, as we recognize its profound impact on personal healing and liberation. Our forgiveness training programs provide a safe and nurturing space for you to explore the  power of forgiveness, both toward others and yourself. You’ll discover how forgiveness can release emotional burdens, foster inner peace, and open the door to deeper connections and personal growth. 
 Whether you are seeking personal transformation, emotional healing, or a greater sense of self-awareness, WalkPono is here to support you every step of the way. Our experienced and compassionate team is dedicated to creating a warm and inclusive environment, where you can explore, learn, and grow. 
 Thank you for visiting WalkPono.org. We look forward to accompanying you on your path to self-discovery and personal growth.”
